\begin{abstract}
We instrument \texttt{TransientBrokerage.jl} --- a base-model matching-market ABM --- to measure when a central broker provides \emph{pair-conditioned informational} value to agents, as distinct from a pure \emph{data-pooling} or \emph{capacity} advantage. Standard holdout metrics conflate the three. We add (i) a decoupled fee/cost split, (ii) per-period demand/fill/value counters with an algebraic decomposition into quality-selection, fill-access, and fee-cost components, (iii) pre-match access/assessment flags, (iv) seven ablation modes --- including a \textsc{BlindBroker} that zeros the focal-agent input while preserving pooled training, (v) pooled and within-agent de-meaned holdout $R^2$/rank metrics, and (vi) a two-way residualized holdout metric that isolates the pair-specific signal. Applied to diagnostic cells at $N{=}1000, T{=}200$, the instrumentation shows that the broker's apparent advantage under a degenerate additively-separable DGP ($\rho{=}1$, "EasyQuality") is 94\% a data-pooling artifact, while at the regime-gated anchors (Default, HardPairwise) pair-conditioned input drives most of the gap. The existing scripts remain byte-identical on the canonical seed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Base model}
\label{sec:model}
Each period $t$ a population of $N$ agents with types $\xvec_i \in \mathbb{R}^d$ on the unit sphere demand matches. For a pair $(i,j)$ the deterministic match signal is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:match}
f(\xvec_i,\xvec_j) \;=\; \underbrace{\rho \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(\xvec_i^\top \cvec + \xvec_j^\top \cvec\right)}_{\text{additive quality}}
\;+\; \underbrace{(1-\rho)\cdot g(\xvec_i,\xvec_j)\cdot \xvec_i^\top A\,\xvec_j}_{\text{gain-modulated interaction}},
\end{equation}
with the regime gain
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:gain}
g(\xvec_i,\xvec_j) \;=\; 1 + \delta\,\operatorname{sign}(\xvec_i^\top B\,\xvec_j).
\end{equation}
$A$ is SPD, $B$ is symmetric and weighted-orthogonalised against $A$, $\cvec$ is an "ideal type" drawn from a sinusoidal curve, $\rho \in [0,1]$ trades additive quality against pairwise interaction, and $\delta \in [0,1]$ controls the magnitude of the two-regime gate. Realised output is $q_{ij} = Q + f(\xvec_i,\xvec_j) + \varepsilon_{ij}$ with $\varepsilon_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_\varepsilon^2)$ and offset $Q = 1$. Calibration fixes $q_{\mathrm{cal}} = \Ebb[Q + f]$ by Monte Carlo and $r = 0.6\,q_{\mathrm{cal}}$ is the outside option. Original default: $(N,T,T_{\mathrm{burn}},d,s,k,\rho,\delta,\sigma_x,\sigma_\varepsilon)=(1000,200,30,8,8,6,0.5,0.5,0.5,0.1)$; simplified screening uses $(N,T,d,k)=(300,120,6,4)$.

Each period every agent draws $d_i \sim \operatorname{Bin}(K{-}m_i, p_{\mathrm{demand}})$ demand slots where $m_i$ is already-active matches and capacity $K{=}5$. Agent $i$ chooses channel $\in\{\texttt{:self},\texttt{:broker}\}$ by comparing EWMA satisfaction indices. Self-search draws $n_\text{strangers}$ random non-neighbours plus all neighbours; the broker offers its current accessible roster ranked by a neural-network prediction over the pair input $(\xvec_i,\xvec_j) \in \mathbb{R}^{2d}$. Round-by-round deferred acceptance finalises matches.

\section{Instrumentation changes}
\label{sec:instrumentation}
All edits are additive and backward-compatible: the canonical preflight (\texttt{quick\_diagnostic.jl} at \texttt{seed=42}) produces \emph{byte-identical} numbers pre- and post-instrumentation (outsourcing rate $0.266$, $R^2$ gap $0.426$).

\subsection{Decoupled fee and cost rates}
Legacy model used a single \texttt{search\_cost\_rate} $s$ for both the broker fee and the self-search cost:
\[
\phi = c_s = s\,(q_{\mathrm{cal}} - r).
\]
The broker-advantage analysis requires separating these. We add two parameters, with defaults that inherit from $s$:
\begin{align}
\phi &= s_{\mathrm{broker}}\,(q_{\mathrm{cal}}-r), & s_{\mathrm{broker}} &\leftarrow \texttt{broker\_fee\_rate}\text{ (default }s\text{)},\\
c_s  &= s_{\mathrm{self}}  \,(q_{\mathrm{cal}}-r), & s_{\mathrm{self}} &\leftarrow \texttt{self\_search\_cost\_rate}\text{ (default }s\text{)}.
\end{align}
Agent satisfaction on the \emph{self} channel updates as $\tilde q^{\text{self}} = \sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}_i^\text{self}} q_m / d_i - c_s$; on the \emph{broker} channel as $\tilde q^{\text{broker}} = (\sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}_i^\text{brk}} q_m - \phi\,|\mathcal{M}_i^\text{brk,std}|)/d_i$. Both use \emph{demanded slots} $d_i$ as denominator; the self-search cost is charged on every demanded slot whether filled or not, while $\phi$ is charged only on successful standard broker placements. EWMA update $\operatorname{Sat} \leftarrow (1-\omega)\operatorname{Sat} + \omega\,\tilde q$ with $\omega=0.2$.

\subsection{Demand, fill, and value counters; algebraic decomposition}
We add, per period:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \texttt{self\_demand\_slots} and \texttt{broker\_demand\_slots} ($d^s, d^b$), with $d^s + d^b = $ \texttt{total\_demand}.
\item \texttt{self\_filled\_slots} and \texttt{broker\_filled\_slots} ($n^s, n^b$), equal to the existing \texttt{n\_self\_matches} and \texttt{n\_broker\_standard}.
\item Fill rates $\pi^s = n^s/d^s$, $\pi^b = n^b/d^b$, gap $\Delta_\pi = \pi^b - \pi^s$.
\item Gross per-demanded-slot value $\bar q^s = (\sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}^s} q_m)/d^s$, similarly $\bar q^b$.
\item Conditional mean $\bar q^s_\text{cond} = (\sum q_m)/n^s$, similarly for broker.
\end{itemize}
Net per-demanded-slot value is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:net}
\nu^s = \bar q^s - c_s, \qquad \nu^b = \bar q^b - \phi\,\pi^b, \qquad \Delta_\nu = \nu^b - \nu^s,
\end{equation}
and admits the algebraic decomposition
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:decomp}
\Delta_\nu \;=\; \underbrace{\pi^b\bigl(\bar q^b_\text{cond} - \bar q^s_\text{cond}\bigr)}_{\text{quality-selection}}
\;+\; \underbrace{\bigl(\pi^b - \pi^s\bigr)\bar q^s_\text{cond}}_{\text{fill/access}}
\;+\; \underbrace{c_s - \pi^b\phi}_{\text{fee/cost}}.
\end{equation}
We verify this identity to floating-point tolerance on every run (max residual observed: $4.44{\times}10^{-16}$). Period-level agent welfare is $W^{(a)}_t = \sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}_t} q_m - c_s d^s_t - \phi n^b_t$; broker revenue $W^{(b)}_t = \phi n^b_t$; both cumulated post-burn.

\subsection{Pre-match access/assessment classification}
For every accepted brokered match $(i,j)$ we record flags \emph{before} the match-edge and partner-history mutations:
\begin{align*}
\mathsf{edge\_pre} &= \mathbb{1}\{j \in N_G(i)\text{ before this match}\},\\
\mathsf{prior\_pair\_history\_pre} &= \mathbb{1}\{\text{partner\_count}[i,j] > 0\}.
\end{align*}
Matches partition into three disjoint classes:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Class & Definition & Spec interpretation\\
\midrule
\texttt{access\_new\_edge} & $\neg\mathsf{edge\_pre}$ & broker bridges a structural hole \\
\texttt{assessment\_reachable\_no\_prior} & $\mathsf{edge\_pre}\wedge\neg\mathsf{prior}$ & broker ranks a reachable stranger \\
\texttt{assessment\_prior\_partner} & $\mathsf{prior}$ & broker ranks a known partner \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Ablation modes}
An \texttt{ablation::Symbol} parameter ($\in$\{\texttt{:none}, \texttt{:NoBroker}, \texttt{:NoRegime}, \texttt{:BlindBroker}, \texttt{:NoAccessBroker}, \texttt{:FrozenGraph}, \texttt{:NoTurnover}, \texttt{:FreezeBrokerLearning}\}) gates the model. \texttt{:NoBroker} is the paired counterfactual for every treatment run (forces $\texttt{channel}\leftarrow\texttt{:self}$ for every demander \emph{after} calling \texttt{outsourcing\_decision} so the RNG state is preserved). \texttt{:NoRegime} sets $\delta \leftarrow 0$ at env construction. \texttt{:FrozenGraph} suppresses the \texttt{add\_match\_edge!} call in \texttt{finalize\_accepted\_proposal!}. \texttt{:NoTurnover} skips entry/exit. \texttt{:FreezeBrokerLearning} retains the initial warm-up training but skips \texttt{train\_broker\_nn!} during production periods. \texttt{:NoAccessBroker} filters broker candidates to pairs with $j \in N_G(i)$.

\textsc{BlindBroker} is the key diagnostic: the broker NN architecture stays at input dimension $2d$, but at every prediction and training call the focal-agent half is zeroed. Concretely,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:blind}
\hat q^{\text{blind}}(\xvec_i,\xvec_j) \;=\; \operatorname{NN}_b(\mathbf{0}_d,\xvec_j),
\end{equation}
so the broker retains its cross-agent pooled training data (same width $h_b$, same history) but has no pair-specific input. Under \textsc{BlindBroker} the training uses symmetry-augmented examples $\{(\mathbf{0},\xvec_j,q),(\mathbf{0},\xvec_i,q)\}$.

\texttt{:EqualCapacityBroker} is deferred; the seven implemented ablations cover the decomposition the current analysis needs.

\subsection{Holdout metrics: within-agent, pooled, de-meaned, residualized}
The existing per-agent holdout samples $n_a=100$ focal agents and $n_p=40$ random partners per agent. We report four gap flavours:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{Within-agent rank/R²}: per-agent Spearman $\rho$ and per-agent $R^2$ on that agent's holdout, averaged across sampled agents. $R^2$ denominator is $\sum (q_j - \overline{q}_i)^2$ where $\overline{q}_i$ is the within-agent mean.
\item \emph{Pooled rank/R²}: Spearman and $R^2$ on all $n_a n_p$ triples concatenated; global mean replaces $\overline{q}_i$. Pooled-$R^2$ punishes a predictor that fails to track per-agent shifts.
\item \emph{Within-agent de-meaned $R^2$}: for each sampled $i$, subtract within-agent means from both truth and prediction before computing $R^2$ on residuals; average. Formally
\[
R^2_{\text{dm},i} = 1 - \frac{\sum_j ((\hat q_{ij}-\overline{\hat q}_i) - (q_{ij}-\overline q_i))^2}{\sum_j (q_{ij}-\overline q_i)^2}.
\]
Removes any per-agent offset advantage.
\item \emph{Two-way residualized $R^2$/rank}: a \emph{fixed} partner sample of $n_p=40$ is drawn once per period (via a side-car RNG seeded from $(\text{seed},t)$ so the main stream is unperturbed; this preserves the \texttt{seed=42} byte-equivalence). All $\min(50,N)$ sampled focal agents are evaluated on the same partner set, giving an estimable (focal $\times$ partner) matrix $Q$. We subtract the truth's two-way main effects
\[
q_{ij} - \hat\mu - \hat\alpha_i - \hat\beta_j
\]
from both truth and predictor, and compute $R^2$ and Spearman on the \emph{pair-specific residual}. A predictor that captures only additive (row/column) structure scores $\approx 0$; only pair-conditioned signal shows up.
\end{enumerate}
The residualized metrics are masked to \texttt{NaN} when the pair-specific variance falls below $\max(\sigma_\varepsilon^2\,n_p/6,10^{-8})\cdot n_p$ \emph{and} below $10^{-6}\cdot \operatorname{Var}(Q)$ (handles the additively separable $\rho=1$ case).

\subsection{Full/Blind/Agent additive decomposition}
For any cell with both a \texttt{:none} run (full broker) and a \texttt{:BlindBroker} run at the same parameters, we report
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:fullblind}
\operatorname{gap}_{\text{full}} \;=\; \underbrace{\operatorname{gap}_{\text{blind}}}_{\text{pooled-data component}}
\;+\; \underbrace{\operatorname{gap}_{\text{full}} - \operatorname{gap}_{\text{blind}}}_{\text{pair-input component}}
\end{equation}
separately for within-rank, demeaned-$R^2$, residualized-rank, and residualized-$R^2$. By construction the "agent-alone gap" in each flavour is zero (it is the reference), so the two components are interpretable in absolute terms.

\subsection{Broker fill-failure reason buckets}
Unfilled broker-channel slots are attributed to two causes observable from the round-matching workspace:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \texttt{broker\_unfilled\_pref\_exhausted}: demander walked through its complete admissible preference list without holding a final proposal.
\item \texttt{broker\_unfilled\_capacity\_limited}: demander retained untried preferences but max rounds were reached --- typically partners filled by earlier proposers.
\end{itemize}
The two counters partition $\texttt{broker\_demand\_slots}-\texttt{broker\_filled\_slots}$ exactly (verified in a smoke test).

\paragraph{Interpretation.} At \texttt{EasyQuality} ($\rho=1$) the DGP~\eqref{eq:match} is additively separable so \emph{no} pair-specific signal exists; accordingly rank$_{\text{resid}}$ is near zero for both arms ($+$0.022 full, $-$0.006 blind). \subsection{Stage~3: Phase~C3 main sweeps at simplified scale ($N{=}300, T{=}120$, 15 seeds/arm, treatment $+$ \texttt{:NoBroker} control)}
\label{sec:c3}

Four 2D sweeps over the base-model parameter space: \texttt{delta\_rho}, \texttt{fee\_cost}, \texttt{transparency\_access}, \texttt{learning\_turnover}. \paragraph{Regime classifier.} For each cell the classifier of \texttt{scripts/run\_broker\_advantage.jl:classify\_regime} uses treatment-arm post-burn means and paired $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item $\pi_b{>}0.50$, $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}{>}0$, rank\_gap${>}0$ $\Rightarrow$ \textsc{BrokerDominant}. Subtype by access\_new\_edge\_frac\_brk: $>$0.60 $\to$ Access (\texttt{BA}); $<$0.30 $\to$ Assessment (\texttt{BI}); else Dual (\texttt{BD}).
\item $\pi_b{>}0.50$, $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}{<}0$ $\Rightarrow$ \textsc{RentExtraction} (\texttt{RE}).
\item $\pi_b{<}0.20$, $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}{>}0$, rank\_gap${>}0$ $\Rightarrow$ \textsc{Underadopted\_Info} (\texttt{UI}).
\item $\pi_b{<}0.20$, rank\_gap${>}0$, gross\_gap${>}0$, $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}\nless 0$ $\Rightarrow$ \textsc{FeeInducedBypass} (\texttt{FB}).
\item $\pi_b{<}0.20$, $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}\nless 0$, rank\_gap$\nless 0$ $\Rightarrow$ \textsc{SelfSearchDominant} (\texttt{SS}).
\item Otherwise \textsc{Mixed} (\texttt{M}).
\end{itemize}
Threshold-robustness maps at $\pi_b{=}0.30$ and $\pi_b{=}0.70$ are generated for \texttt{delta\_rho} and \texttt{fee\_cost}.

\paragraph{Data integrity caveat on residualized $R^2$.} At simplified-scale $(N{=}300, T{=}120)$ the NaN-gate on pair-residualized $R^2$ fails at $\rho\in\{0.75,1.00\}$: \texttt{r2\_gap\_residualized\_mean} is reported as $+1.34$ to $+1.85$ for $\rho{=}0.75$ and $+33.06$ (SE 4.48) for \emph{all} four $\rho{=}1.00$ cells in \texttt{delta\_rho\_cell\_summaries.csv}; true $R^2\in(-\infty,1]$, so these values exceed range. Also note: at $\rho{=}1$ the DGP~\eqref{eq:match} is $\delta$-invariant, so cells \texttt{d0.00\_r1.00}, \texttt{d0.25\_r1.00}, \texttt{d0.50\_r1.00}, \texttt{d0.75\_r1.00} produce byte-identical summaries.

\paragraph{Key Stage~4 findings strictly from the CSV cells cited.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Magnitude amplification.} At \texttt{broker\_dominant}, $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}$ grows from $+364.7$ (SE 648, not statistically significant at simplified scale) to $+16614.0$ (SE 2152, clearly $>0$) at full scale --- a $\sim$45$\times$ amplification. \item \textbf{Fee-induced bypass signature confirmed.} At \texttt{fee\_bypass}, full-scale $\pi_b{=}0.136$ is below the 0.20 threshold for \textsc{FeeInducedBypass}, rank\_gap is $+0.107$, and $\Delta W_{\text{ag}}$ is $-6297.5$ (SE 1590). \end{itemize}

\section{Summary of findings}
\label{sec:summary}

Claims in this section cite a specific CSV row, figure, or identity residual. ``Pending'' marks items awaiting completion of still-running sweeps or Stage~4.

\paragraph{(i) Pooled-data vs.\ pair-input decomposition.} At the full-scale diagnostic (\texttt{diagnostic\_cell\_summaries.csv}; Phase~C2c blind comparison, Table~\ref{tab:c2c}):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item In the \textbf{Default} cell ($\delta{=}0.5,\rho{=}0.5$), \textsc{BlindBroker} (pooled data, no pair input) scores rank$_{\text{residualized}}{=}-0.605$ while Full scores $+0.074$ (Table~\ref{tab:c2c}). The pair-input component (Full $-$ Blind, eq.~\eqref{eq:fullblind}) is $+0.679$ in residualized-rank units --- pair-conditioned signal is the dominant driver here.
\item In \textbf{EasyQuality} ($\rho{=}1$, additively separable DGP), rank$_{\text{residualized}}$ is near zero for both arms ($+0.022$ Full, $-0.006$ Blind) as theory predicts, yet within-agent rank\_gap is $+0.249$ Full vs.\ $+0.235$ Blind --- Blind retains 94\% of the within-agent rank advantage. The broker's apparent edge here is data pooling (cross-agent $\sim$60\,000 observations vs.\ $\sim$60 per agent), not information asymmetry.
\item In \textbf{HardPairwise} ($\delta{=}0.75,\rho{=}0.25$), Blind collapses to rank$_{\text{residualized}}{=}-0.622$ while Full stays at $+0.060$ --- pair input is again essential; the small Full value reflects the $h_b{=}32$ broker NN's capacity shortfall on the discontinuous regime gate.
\end{itemize}
These full-scale decompositions are the reliable reference; simplified-scale residualized-$R^2$ in \texttt{delta\_rho} is gate-broken at high $\rho$ (§\ref{sec:c3}) and cannot be used for analogous claims on the sweep grid. Residualized-\emph{rank}, bounded, remains usable at simplified scale.

\section{Findings so far and open items}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Sanity criterion "rank\_gap shrinks under NoRegime / EasyQuality" is a spec expectation based on equal-data asymptotics. At realistic finite $(N,T)$ the broker's pooled-data advantage dominates any loss of pair-conditioned structure. The instrumentation is sound --- the preflight baseline is byte-identical to the pre-instrumentation run on \texttt{seed=42}.
\item \textbf{Net value gap $\Delta_\nu$ is negative in every cell examined} ($-0.07$ to $-0.30$). At default $\phi$, broker use is not net-beneficial to agents on a per-demanded-slot basis even where prediction is better. This motivates the \texttt{fee\_cost} and \texttt{transparency\_access} sweeps.
\item The \textsc{BlindBroker}-Full pairwise decomposition~\eqref{eq:fullblind} cleanly separates pooled-data from pair-input advantages and will be read alongside the regime map for every main-sweep cell at the anchor rows.
\item Pilot criterion (2) --- "rank\_gap near zero in no-regime / pure-quality anchor" --- does not hold strictly at simplified scale; 4/5 criteria pass. Main sweeps launched regardless, conditional on Stage~4 full-scale confirmation.
\item \textbf{Known caveat.} Residualized $R^2$ gate is miscalibrated at simplified scale for $\rho{\ge}0.75$; use \texttt{rank\_gap\_residualized} there instead (see §\ref{sec:c3}). Full-scale residualized $R^2$ is correctly NaN-gated (\texttt{diagnostic\_cell\_summaries.csv} \texttt{EasyQuality} row).
\end{enumerate}
